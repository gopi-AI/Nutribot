# Kidney-Friendly Nutrition
- Emphasize **low-sodium**, **adequate protein**, and **controlled potassium** depending on CKD stage.
- Prefer fruits like apples, berries, grapes; limit high-potassium fruits (bananas, oranges) if potassium is elevated.
- Choose unsalted whole grains, olive oil, and herbs/spices over salt.
- Consult a renal dietitian for individualized targets.
